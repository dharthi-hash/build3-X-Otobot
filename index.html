04QUESTIONS

Things founders
often ask.

Short, honest answers. If something here doesn't cover it, send the question over — we'd rather have the conversation.

  1. Earlier than feels comfortable. If you've got a clear problem and a sense of who's hurting from it, that's enough. We do our best work before the deck exists.

  2. Early-stage startups operate differently, so we shape the engagement to fit the stage, scope, and long-term vision. We're open to upfront project-based pricing, hybrid equity + execution, or milestone-based structures. The goal is a setup that works for both sides and lets the product actually move forward.

  3. Yes, for the right founder. We've taken cash, equity, and mixes of both. We don't use equity as a discount lever — only when we genuinely want skin in the game alongside you.

  4. Software with a real interface. Consumer apps, vertical SaaS, marketplaces, internal tools that became products. If a customer is going to log in and do something, we're a good fit.

  5. We stay in our lane. We don't take on deep-tech R&D, hardware products, manufacturing-heavy systems, scientific or research-led product development, or highly regulated enterprise infrastructure. If that's your world, we'll happily point you toward someone better suited.

  6. You own everything — code, design files, infrastructure, accounts. We can stay on for a few weeks of polish, hand off to your team, or help you hire the right first engineer. Your call.

  7. Depends on the problem. Simple consumer flows can ship in weeks; deeper platform work takes longer. We won't pad a timeline to look fast, and we won't promise something we can't actually ship.

  8. We've been founders, designers, and engineers — not consultants. We make the decisions you wish you had a second pair of hands for, and we ship instead of advising.
